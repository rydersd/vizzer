# Consolidate reusable documentation in the wiki

> Status: shipped
> Deps: []
> Release: R1
> Tags: wiki, documentation

## Intent

Make the wiki the maintained home for useful operating guides, design concepts,
and historical lessons that were scattered under docs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Review every docs Markdown page and record a destination or retention reason.
- Move substantive knowledge into organized wiki guides, concepts and history.
- Preserve dated claims and distinguish historical records from current contracts.
- Keep old links usable without duplicating forwarding pages in the work graph.
- Update the wiki index, current links, source map and product documentation.

## Evidence — 2026-09-05

Nine pages migrated. [Migration inventory](../../wiki/documentation-map.md)
covers all former docs pages. Ordered source-line comparison confirmed no
original content was lost (link destinations rebased; contextual notices added).
Four retained evidence/plan files remain byte-identical. Local links resolve,
all nine destinations are graph references, and none of the forwarding pages
is indexed. Refresh/check passed without conflicts or warnings.
